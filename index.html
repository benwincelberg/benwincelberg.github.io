Working Papers

  1. Risk Aversion Reversals Job Market Paper
    with Po Hyun Sung
    Extended Abstract at EC '26. Awarded Best Third Year Paper, HSS Caltech (2025)

    Which stochastic choice models preserve the connection between risk aversion in preferences and behavior, and which reverse it?

  2. Monotonicity and Bracketing in Games
    with Fedor Sandomirskiy, Po Hyun Sung, and Omer Tamuz
    Revise & Resubmit, Journal of Political Economy

    When players favor stochastically dominating options given each other’s behavior and consider each game in isolation, play resembles Nash or logit quantal response equilibrium

  3. A Separability Foundation for Random Coefficients Logit
    with Fedor Sandomirskiy, Po Hyun Sung, and Omer Tamuz
    Conditionally Accepted, American Economic Review

    Random coefficients logit is the only rule whose predictions are unaffected by irrelevant decisions

  4. Robustness to Delegation in Games
    with Fedor Sandomirskiy
    Extended Abstract at EC '26

    Which strategic environments are robust to players delegating decisions to AI agents, cartel managers, and other intermediaries?

  5. Measuring Economic Preferences in the Presence of Noise: The Connections Between Choices and Valuations
    with Ted O'Donoghue, Charles Sprenger, and Po Hyun Sung

    When do differences between choices and valuations reveal unstable preferences, and when can noise explain them?
